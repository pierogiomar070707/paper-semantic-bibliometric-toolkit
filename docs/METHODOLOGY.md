# Methodological Architecture

## Input
A complete Scopus CSV containing citation information, bibliographical information, abstracts, Author Keywords, Index Keywords, affiliations, funding, and references.

## Pipeline
1. Corpus integrity and metadata coverage.
2. Conservative text and source normalization.
3. Keyword frequencies and co-occurrence networks.
4. Domain knowledge extraction through transparent dictionaries and TF-IDF discovery.
5. Temporal trends and emerging terms.
6. Sources, authors, institutions, countries, and collaboration.
7. Citation indicators and Related Work candidate ranking.
8. Audited thesaurus and VOSviewer protocol.
9. Vector figures, paper evidence report, and final QA.

## Interpretation rule
Every automatically generated result is descriptive or exploratory. Scientific claims require triangulation with the underlying titles, abstracts, full papers, and targeted literature searches.
